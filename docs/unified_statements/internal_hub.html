Gross Revenue Deal Mechanics & Recoupment Calculation Rules

In Gross Revenue Share agreements, author royalties are calculated directly on gross receipts collected from user transactions, after subtracting only third-party Payment Gateway (PG) fees. Marketing spends, narrator fees, and platform infrastructure overhead are entirely absorbed by Pocket FM and are not deducted from the author's gross share.

1. Gross Revenue Calculation
Gross Rev excl. PG = Revenue incl. PG - PG Charges
Excludes third-party payment gateway transaction processing charges.
2. Author Revenue Share
Author Rev Share = Gross Rev excl. PG × Contract Rev Share %
Calculated on contracted tier (typically 15% or 20%).
3. Recoupment & Payout Release
MG Recouped = min(Author Share, Opening MG)
Closing MG = Opening MG - MG Recouped
Author Revenue Share Payable remains $0.00 until Opening MG reaches $0.00.

Net Revenue Deal Mechanics & Capped Production Deductions

In Net Revenue Share agreements, designated direct expenses (production narration, audio engineering, marketing spends) are deductible before calculating author royalty distributions. Pocket FM strictly enforces the Production Cost Deduction Cap Rule to guarantee author equity.

1. Net Share Base Formula
Net Rev for Share = Gross Rev excl. PG - Deductible Prod Costs - Spends
Direct expenses are subtracted from gross revenue to determine the net profit pool.
2. Production Cost Cap Rule
Deductible Costs = min(Reported Costs, Gross Rev excl. PG)
Deductible production costs are strictly capped at gross revenue. Net revenue cannot fall below $0.00 and cannot generate author debt.
3. Recoupment & Payout Release
Author Share = Net Base × 15%
Closing MG = Opening MG - MG Recouped
Author Revenue Share Payable releases only once Opening MG is fully recouped.
